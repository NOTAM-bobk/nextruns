The daily trainer category is crowded, and most of what separates the good ones from the forgettable ones isn't any single spec — it's how little you think about the shoe over hundreds of miles. That's where this one stands out.

## The ride

The midsole strikes a middle ground: enough cushioning to make long runs comfortable, without the mushy, disconnected feel that makes some max-cushion trainers feel vague at faster paces. It's genuinely capable of handling everything from a recovery jog to a moderate tempo session, which is exactly what a "daily trainer" should mean.

## Fit and upper

The engineered mesh upper is snug through the midfoot without pressure points, and the toe box has enough room for feet to splay naturally on longer efforts — something that matters more the further you run. True to size for most runners; if you're between sizes, we'd lean down rather than up given the accommodating toe box.

## Durability

After putting real mileage on this shoe, the outsole rubber is holding up well in high-wear zones, and the midsole foam hasn't noticeably compressed or lost bounce. This is shaping up to be a shoe that goes the distance most daily trainers promise but don't always deliver.

## Who it's for

Runners who want one shoe in the rotation that can handle the bulk of a training week — easy days, long runs, and the occasional moderate effort — without needing a separate shoe for each. It's not a racing shoe and doesn't pretend to be; if you're looking for a dedicated speed shoe, look at a lighter, more responsive option instead.

## Verdict

A reliable, well-balanced daily trainer that does the unglamorous job of a daily trainer better than most. Not the flashiest shoe in the lineup, but likely to be the one you keep coming back to.
